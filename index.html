How to Play

Each round, the enemy commits to a move, then a 5-second countdown starts. Pick your answer before it reaches zero. You can change your mind until then. Shield is chosen for you if you don't pick.

  • Shield (green) blocks red attacks.
    Example: Shield beats the Orc's Cleave.
  • Sword (red) cuts through yellow shouts.
    Example: Sword beats the Dragon's Roar.
  • Battlecry (yellow) shatters green guards.
    Example: Battlecry beats the Skeleton's Bone Shield.
  • = Same colour is a draw. Nobody is hurt.

Winning a round removes one enemy heart. Losing one costs you a heart. You have 5 hearts, and they are refilled before every encounter.

Enemies repeat patterns. Easy foes show their next move. Medium foes reveal it in the last 2 seconds. Hard foes only show a pose and a small coloured glint. Learn the poses, and use the Last enemy moves list to spot the rhythm.

Controls: click or tap the ability buttons, or press 1 2 3. Press Esc or P to pause.
